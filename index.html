From the cashew apple to your pouch

A cashew grows outside its fruit, one nut hanging below each cashew apple. Here's what happens before it reaches you.

1

Bloom

Trees flower in the dry season and set one nut under every apple.

2

Harvest

Ripe apples drop. The nuts are twisted off and sun dried.

3

Shell & peel

Steamed, cracked open and peeled to free the ivory kernel.

4

Grade

Sorted by hand into whole, splits and pieces.

5

Grind & pack

Some kernels are ground into powder, then everything is sealed in 1 kg pouches.
